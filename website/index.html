The short version

Questions, answered.

KronTerm is an AI-native developer workspace that combines a terminal, browser, files, sandboxes, previews, and agents in one composable canvas.

KronosCode is KronTerm’s agent engine. It understands the workspace, coordinates specialist agents, and operates terminals, files, browsers, and blocks through explicit tools and approval boundaries.

Yes. KronTerm is designed for bring-your-own-key providers including OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini, plus local models through Ollama and LM Studio.

KronTerm is built for macOS, Windows, and Linux, with a shared Electron interface and a native Go backend.

KronTerm is under active development. Join the community or follow the build for verified release updates—we will not send you to placeholder installers.
